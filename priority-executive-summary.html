Prospective work / no lane assigned

Four ranked, previously proposed-but-unrun mechanisms.

Qualitative experiment priority only.
No numeric score estimate or run authorization.

1. CSR-2 regime-gated experts

Combine a piedmont scarp expert with a cover-blind magnetic/gravity/MT expert under one frozen regional gate. This differs from CSR-1’s single global model; mimics include eroded fan scarps, lava fronts, lithologic boundaries and survey seams. Low–medium modeling cost after restoration; the pinned inputs are not present in this checkout.

2. GeoDAWN alteration-ratio persistence

Test K/Th/U log-ratio residuals with cross-line persistence and a within-survey-block null. Mimics include soil/lithology, moisture and flight-line seams. The official source is recorded as CC0 in an earlier receipt, but the pinned TIFFs are missing now and metadata were not rechecked; not ready.

3. Drainage deflection across a field edge

Measure repeated channel offsets and knickpoint alignments from official 3DEP/NHD, then require independent magnetic/gravity support. This differs from LiDAR scarp amplitude. Mimics include terraces, roads, lava dams and lithology. A prior query covered one test bbox only; full ROI bytes/coverage are unverified, so not viable yet.

4. GeMS/SGMC unit-contact discordance

Project geology-unit contacts beneath alluvium and compare with independent geophysical edges, excluding fault lines. Mimics include unconformities and unfaulted stratigraphic terminations. The updated official GeMS product and unit polygons are not locally verified; acquisition and GIS cost is medium–high, so not viable yet.

Protocol & sources

What happens before a future slot.

  1. Obtain a single assigned scientific lane and an explicit resolution of the literal registry constraint; do not reduce the budget merely to evade it.
  2. Pre-register whole-segment, spatially buffered hide-and-recover folds; use visible faults only for catalogue-derived features, and audit each feature for leakage.
  3. Beat the current same-protocol control with uncertainty, check surface and final dots against the complete current registry, and validate the exact saved bytes.
  4. Require separate slot-selector approval. A missing gate, incomplete inventory or local format pass is not an upload authorization.
