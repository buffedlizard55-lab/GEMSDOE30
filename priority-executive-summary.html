Why GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 is not the answer

It is D2.8 minus the ≤2 px catalogue-halo dots: removing near-catalogue dots shrinks the masked-domain false-positive mass while keeping true-positive credit, raising the Tversky. But "-zeros" writes 0 outside the footprint, which the portal rejects ("Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"), and it is not unique. A format-valid successor must use NaN outside and a distinct dot field.
