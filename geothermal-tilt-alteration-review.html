03 / Geothermal vent & blind-fault literature knowledge base

Verified official datasets & geophysical mechanisms.

Structural controls (Great Basin)

75%+ of Great Basin geothermal systems are blind (no surface hot spring/fumarole), hosted in fault step-overs, relay ramps, terminations, and intersections where localized dilation maintains fracture permeability beneath Playas/Quaternary alluvium (Faulds & Hinz, 2015, WGC; Coolbaugh et al., 2006).

GDR 1391 INGENIOUS compilation (DOI 10.15121/1881483) ↗

Potential-field tilt-depth & upward continuation

Salem et al. (2007, Leading Edge 26:1502) prove magnetic tilt angle θ = atan2(∂M/∂z, |∇_h M|) satisfies θ = 0 over vertical fault contacts and h_tilt = 1 / |∇_h θ| estimates depth to buried fault top independent of magnetization amplitude. FFT upward continuation isolates basement feeders from surficial volcanic clutter.

USGS GeoDAWN (Glen & Earney 2024, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ) ↗

Potassic alteration, MT clay-cap & 1 m LiDAR

Ascending K/Si-rich hydrothermal fluids deposit adularia/illite along fault damage zones while Th stays immobile, elevating fold-regressed K_d and Efimov F = K·eU/eTh (Gnojek & Přichystal, 1985), flanked by smectite clay-cap MT conductance gradients (Cumming, 2009) and subtle 1 m LiDAR dip-slip scarps.
