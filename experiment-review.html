Why H33 may have improved—and why that is not a forecast.

For weighted truth credit T, false-positive mass F and truth count G, the shared metric is DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2F + 0.8G + ε). Credit is a maximum over a triangular neighborhood, not an unrestricted sum. Removing redundant dots can save FP mass with little loss in truth coverage. Catalogue-flank pruning can also delete genuine new splays; its hidden-test effect cannot be inferred from support alone.

The owner's 0.2778 attribution and conflicting leaderboard claims remain unverified historical claims, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. All 119 supplied claims remain quarantined. No measured evidence here supports a promise to exceed them.
