One hypothesis was promoted on a spatial holdout, one was falsified, and both receipts are published. No number on this page is an organizer score.

01 / Registered design

Hide-and-recover over whole fault components.

Whole 8-connected catalogue components are withheld as stand-in “new” faults; the remaining components are masked exactly as the organizer masks known faults. Every catalogue-derived quantity is computed only from the visible half. Emissions are binary, Poisson-disk thinned at 300 m, and scored with the exact masked distance-weighted Tversky index (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, triangular 300 m kernel), pooling truth credit and false-positive mass before the ratio is taken.

Controls. The primary arm is compared against (a) a proximity-matched random emission whose distance-to-known profile matches the candidate's, and (b) a uniform off-catalogue emission. A positive result therefore cannot be explained by generic proximity to mapped faults, nor by the mere absence of a geographic prior.

Zone definition (frozen). Dots sit 300 m–1.5 km from known traces, scored by inverse distance to the known set, so the inner edge starts immediately outside mapped traces. The inner exclusion band is what allows a dot to fall within 300 m of a withheld component without landing on mapped pixels.

02 / The negative experiment

H-VENT: a real hypothesis that the proxy could not validate.

Volcanic feeder fissures are a genuinely uncatalogued population: only 615 of 6,776 vent pixels (≈9%) lie within 300 m of a catalogue trace, and 0 of 21 inventoried vents do. On the catalogue proxy the vent-aligned arm scored 0.000609 against a matched control of 0.018679 on screen and 0.001403 vs 0.016792 on confirmation — decisively worse, 0/4 quadrants, both paired CIs entirely below zero.

This is not evidence that volcanic vents do not mark faults. It is evidence that a catalogue-derived proxy cannot adjudicate a hypothesis about structures the catalogue does not contain (IR-30-017, IR-30-079). The line is closed on this proxy and the negative result is published as a deliverable.

03 / Uniqueness and its limits

Unique against 21 local rasters — with a stated margin.

Against every locally available registry raster the maximum full-raster Spearman correlation is 0.0703 (duplicate threshold 0.90) and the maximum share of candidate dots falling within 3 px of one reference is 68.08% (duplicate threshold 70%). No lane collision. The margin on the overlap statistic is thin, and the complete parallel registry is unavailable here (IR-30-078), so a later session with the full registry must re-run the final-dot comparison before any upload.

An inverse-square inner-edge variant was also tried while building the artifact: it concentrated too hard and collided with a dense existing edge-hybrid reference. The frozen inverse-linear weighting was retained. That ablation is recorded here so the published support is reproducible rather than cherry-picked.

04 / Boundaries

What this is, and what it is not.

  • It is a placement result on a catalogue proxy, not a new fault-physics detector. It does not claim to see faults a magnetometer or a lidar cannot.
  • It is not a leaderboard projection. The reported 0.2778 (GEMSDOE32 H33), 0.2600 (GEMSDOE25/30 D2.8) and 0.4413 (live leaderboard) remain unverified historical claims with no receipt binding a score to a file hash (IR-30-080).
  • It is not geothermal-vent detection. No temperature, permeability, flow or vent observation was used.
  • It does not use organizer-authenticated data: everything ran on SHA-256-pinned owner mirrors (IR-30-001).
