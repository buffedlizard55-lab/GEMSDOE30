01 / PhD analysis of GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) & leaderboard gap (0.3195 → 0.4413)

Why h33-h33-2-b2 topped the GEMSDOE sites—and how to beat it.

Under the official metric DTI = TP_w / (0.2·TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·G + ε) (Problem Description; Forum 11550), truth coverage TP_w is a spatial maximum over a R = 300 m (3 px) triangular kernel, whereas false-positive penalty 0.2 · FP_w sums linearly across unmasked background predictions. DrivenData Staff (Forum 11516/4) confirmed that known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked pixel-exactly (labels == 1 only, zero buffer) while new hidden faults can lie <300 m from mapped traces (Forum 11536).

Byte-exact audit (zero mismatching pixels) proves GEMSDOE32's h33-h33-2-b2 (37,654 dots; 0.2778 owner-reported, unverified) equals GEMSDOE28's h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8 (40,199 dots; 0.2708 owner-reported) after deleting the 2,545 dots at 0 < d_cat ≤ 2 px. Those flank dots were unmasked (FP_w ≈ 1.0 each, saving ~509 weighted penalty units when removed) while surviving dots at d_cat = 3 px still extend triangular kernel support across d_cat ∈ [1, 2] px. Conversely, GEMSDOE36 added 1,200 extra dots onto H33 (38,854 dots → 0.2750 owner-reported), diluting precision.

How to beat 0.2778, 0.3195 (Oct 3 snapshot), and approach 0.4413 (Oct 25 live leader): All 0.2449–0.2778 family rasters place 70–81% of their dots on steep range-front scarps (det_elev_slope ≥ 5°; H33 has only 19.1% of dots in <5° terrain), missing concealed intrabasin geothermal faults beneath Quaternary playas and alluvial fans. Breaking past 0.32–0.44 requires fusing (1) multi-scale steerable piedmont/scarp discontinuity features (MSDB-1v2: HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.17046, +27.6% over core19) upgraded with PTA-1's 1000× tmi_vg unit fix, (2) G30-TAD's Kd-alteration + 30/40/30 decensored collar and PTA-1's low-slope basin specialist (pta_dip_cover_step raw AUC 0.5988), and (3) a 2D multi-km spatial UNet backbone with d_cat > 2 px flank pruning + metric-exact EDGE emission.
