Research register · five hypotheses, one shipped hedge

Five ways to find a fault the catalogue missed.

Ranked by expected DTI gain per unit of implementation cost. Each names exact layers, the physical signature being exploited, why it could reveal a fault absent from the public catalogue, and how it differs from anything already tried here. Full text and equations: hypotheses.md.

1
shipped as a bounded hedge		 USGS State Geologic Map Compilation fault linework (NV + CA), rasterised to the 100 m grid: 82,151 px, 21,160 features, 1,679 components. Signature = mapped trace, used as a distance field plus an emission prior, restricted to the 61,664 px that sit >300 m from every catalogue fault. The scoring catalogue is the USGS Quaternary fault database plus INGENIOUS. The SGMC is a different compilation by different agencies (state geological surveys, 1:24,000–1:250,000), so its fault set is not a subset of the catalogue. A fault digitised by a state survey but absent from the Quaternary database is exactly the class the sponsor's experts would have had available when they labelled "new" faults. Every prior experiment here used the catalogue as truth or as a proxy. No prior work used SGMC as a model input or emission prior. Gain: highest of the five; cost: low (bridge built and running). Delivered — layer committed, 75.1 % of it off-catalogue, two falsification tests run (below).
2 1 m LiDAR scarp morphology from 3DEP DEM: 12 bands including step_max, lapneg_max, lappos_max, downface_max, upface_max, cross_max, relief, coh100, strike. Signature = paired opposite-sign curvature across the local strike (monoclinal scarp: convex crest, concave base) with along-strike coherence filtering. Published maps are compiled from field mapping and aerial photography; scarps shorter than a metre, or degraded and vegetated, are systematically absent from them. The sponsor collected 1 m lidar for this region and states that most faults there are subtle. Prior work used the 100 m det_elev_slope band and ridge transforms. The 1 m-derived curvature pair and the paired-sign test are new. Gain: high; cost: low (layer already mirrored). Registered; not yet ablated against the 19-band baseline.
3 Potential-field derivatives: TMI, RTP, tmi_hg, tmi_vg, iso_grav_anom (+ slopes), vertical derivative, upward continuations at 150–1000 m. Signature = tilt-angle ridge maxima persisting across upward continuations (worming-style), emitted as a smooth per-pixel field rather than binary peaks. The catalogue is dominated by surface-expressed faults. Structures buried under basin fill have no surface trace by definition, and the sponsor explicitly states that many faults in this region are hidden below the surface and require geophysical data to detect. A worming attempt upstream failed because it was emitted as sparse binary peak sets (nonzero on 0.001–0.084 % of pixels) — a coverage failure, not a signal failure. The smooth-field formulation is new here. Gain: medium-high; cost: medium (multi-scale continuation on 2 CPUs). Registered; not yet run.
4 Thermal plumbing: GDR 1391 well & spring temperature/chemistry (temp_c, quartz/chalcedony/cation geothermometers), paleo-geothermal deposits (sinter/tufa), and 2 m temperature-probe surveys. Signature = geothermometry residual (measured vs chemistry-implied temperature) plus anisotropic kernel density of thermal points elongated along the local structure strike. A spring line with no mapped fault is direct surface evidence of an unmapped permeable structure. Sinter and tufa record paleo upflow and survive after the spring dies, extending the record beyond currently active systems. Earlier attempts used coarse thermal-point counts and raw openness; the geothermometry residual and the paleo-deposit layer have not been used. Gain: medium; cost: medium (sparse points must become a field). Source reachable from the CI runner; subset already mirrored. Registered; not yet run.
5 Geodetic strain: geod_shearrate, geod_dilaterate, geod_2ndinv and GDR geodetic grids. Signature = strain localisation index (local second invariant normalised by a long-wavelength background) highlighting narrow deforming corridors. Geodetic strain is depth-integrated and independent of surface expression, so it can flag active structures with no mapped scarp. The bands were previously used raw, and an earlier strain × conductance interaction pilot failed its registered screen — which is why this ranks last, not why it is excluded. Gain: lowest of the five; cost: low (layers in hand). Registered; carries a documented prior negative result.

H1 · corroboration test · positive

2.9× enrichment over chance

24.94 % of SGMC fault pixels lie within 300 m of a public-catalogue fault, against a footprint base rate of 8.61 %. The layer preferentially marks real faults rather than being arbitrary linework.

H1 · independent-evidence test · low power

Lidar scarp AUC 0.52 vs local control

Against partner pixels 1–2 km away matched on distance-to-catalogue and terrain-slope decile, the off-catalogue class is only marginally enriched in 1 m scarp morphology (best band step_max AUC 0.520). The positive control behaves the same way — catalogue faults score ≈0.52 on the same measure — so the test cannot separate real from unreal linework. Recorded as inconclusive, not as a pass.

What the metric says about betting on a hypothesis

Under DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w), adding one prediction pixel whose triangular kernel credit against an uncovered truth pixel is k changes the denominator by exactly 0.2 and the numerator by k. The dot therefore helps iff k > 0.2 × DTI — about 5.2 % at the incumbent's operating point. The rule contains no reference to the size of the hidden truth set, so it does not loosen or tighten as the private set shrinks. This is why a large, public, independent inventory is worth a bounded hedge even though no local frame can score it: the bar for betting is very low, and the layer's downside is structurally capped. Full derivation: metric response surface.

Validation protocol owed for hypotheses 2–5: four buffered spatial folds, identical architecture/seed/budget, separate feature ablations, pooled official DTI components, and near-miss distance bins, with the screen pre-registered before scores are seen. A known-catalogue proxy is not private expert truth.
