1 · H-31-04
Radiometric alteration unmixing along lineaments		 geodawn_rad_u8.tif: K, Th, U, total count; derived Th/K, U/K, U/Th. Test K–Th–U ratio-space alteration plus along-lineament coherence, not raw band magnitude. Fluid-rock alteration may form a halo where no mapped surface trace or scarp exists. A three-endmember/ratio transform coupled to lineament coherence is distinct from using radiometric bands as ordinary model inputs. Ratios may mostly encode lithology, so stratified nulls are mandatory. +0.001…+0.012
Low compute cost; source/schema audit first.		 USGS GeoDAWN, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ. Public-domain catalog record. Local quantized bands are owner-derived mirrors; metadata and source-to-band lineage are not independently authenticated. Checksum and verify bands; freeze unmixing and lineament rules. On four buffered spatial blocks compare against regional baseline, raw-band arm, and count-matched spatial null; require pooled ΔDTI ≥ +0.005 over same-run best, ≥3/4 positive folds and fresh-seed replication, with near-miss and lithology-stratified results reported.
2 · H-32-03
Geothermometer discordance / blind-upflow zoning		 Wellspring chemistry fields geothermquartz_c, geothermchalc_c, geothermcat_c plus cond_surf. Detect non-equilibrium temperature discordance and test corridors on independent lineaments. Chemistry can sample deep pathways above faults without surface expression. This is different from scalar spring-temperature or thermal-population work. The usable chemistry subset is only about 1,000 sites; power and sample-selection bias are concerns. +0.001…+0.012
Low compute cost after source/schema check.		 GDR 1486 records the well/spring mirror provenance; GDR 1391 documents the INGENIOUS compilation. Exact CSV lineage, license, and schema need re-verification; owner mirrors are not organizer-authenticated. Predeclare discordance formula and lineament tolerance. Compare chemistry-only and chemistry×conductance against temperature-only and proximity-matched controls on buffered blocks; require the common +0.005 / 3-of-4 / fresh-seed gate and hold out whole site clusters. Stop if provenance or schema fails.
3 · H-32-02
Quaternary vent-feeder alignment		 21 GDR 1391 vents (20 basalt, one rhyolite) plus tmi_hg/magnetic-anomaly edges. Test two or more vents aligned over 2–8 km and continuation along a dike-like magnetic edge. Vent chains can expose feeder structures absent from the fault catalogue; 0/21 vents are within 300 m of catalogue traces. No inspected local or public owner summary uses vent geometry. Small n and non-tectonic volcanic controls limit power. +0.001…+0.010
Low compute cost; low statistical power.		 GDR 1391 states CC BY 4.0; its Quaternary-volcanics archive was downloaded and hash-verified through the recorded runner bridge. The derived raster remains an owner mirror. Freeze vent-pair distance/strike and magnetic-edge tolerances. Compare with 1,000 spatially constrained random point sets and magnetic-only controls on blocked holdout; require a positive paired effect in ≥3/4 folds and fresh-seed confirmation. Reject rather than widen the search after seeing results.
4 · H-31-03
Drainage-response fault traces		 USGS NHD flowlines plus 1–10 m USGS 3DEP DEM. Seek strike-aligned knickpoint chains, channel offsets/beheaded channels, and channel geometry coherent across a candidate trace. Drainage may preserve deformation where scarps are eroded or buried. No hydrology transform was found in inspected repository or public owner summaries. Must distinguish tectonics from lithology, drainage capture, and DEM artefacts. +0.002…+0.020
Medium-high data/processing cost.		 USGS TNM NHD Best Resolution query returned products for one test bbox only. Full ROI coverage, downloads, reuse details, and DEM linkage remain unchecked. 3DEP is public domain. Obtain full-ROI data; checksum and verify schema/coverage before freezing a detector. Compare with slope-only and spatially matched controls on buffered blocks; require +0.005 over best, ≥3/4 folds and fresh-seed confirmation. Incomplete coverage stops the test.
5 · H-32-04
Kinematically constrained Qfaults gap completion (conditional)		 Quaternary traces with recency, slip sense/rate, and length. Test physically consistent gap-extension/continuity beyond 300 m from catalogue pixels. Potentially adds unmapped geometry, but Qfaults/INGENIOUS provenance overlaps training labels; catalogue-only validation is circular. This differs from scarp morphology by using fault kinematics rather than topography. The corrected Qfaults raster is not independent truth. +0.005…+0.025 conditional
Medium data/analysis cost.		 OSTI 1148722 and GDR 616 are official independent-inventory candidates; pages were checked, but archive transfer, schema, and ROI coverage are unverified. The GDR 1391 Qfaults v2 raster was corrected; 1/59,065 footprint pixels is >300 m from catalogue. Hard gate: retrieve/checksum an inventory; verify independence from INGENIOUS/Qfaults and ROI coverage; then preregister blocked holdout. Require +0.005 over count-matched baseline, ≥3/4 folds, and fresh-seed replication. If no independent truth is available, do not score or promote.

Validation gate

No slot until blocked holdout passes

Freeze source hashes, recipe, thresholds, folds, seed, and count budget before scoring. Compare against the same-run best and spatial/count-matched controls; report both global budget and active scored-domain counts, near-miss bins, and a fresh-seed confirmation. A catalogue-derived frame is a biased proxy, not private expert truth.

Evidence boundary

“Untried” is bounded, not absolute

The shortlist excludes tested variants in the inspected repository and public owner summaries. It cannot establish absence of private experiments. Recheck exact layer manifests and provenance before preregistration; if a candidate duplicates a prior arm, replace it before looking at scores.
