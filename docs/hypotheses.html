Measured outcomes recorded with the register

Loss geometry (core brief question). The boundary-aware objective uses a Euclidean distance transform sampled in metres on the real 300 m kernel radius, added to (not replacing) the regional soft-Tversky term. Paired four-fold buffered spatial holdout, identical recipes, screen budget: pooled exact full-grid OOF DTI 0.062042 → 0.063990 (Δ +0.0019, +3.14 %), 3/4 folds positive, partial-distance-only truth pixels 80 → 40. Fresh-seed (31) confirmation: Δ +0.000115, only 2/4 folds positive (sign-flipping), while partial-distance-only truth still fell 1,889 → 724. The screen did not replicate; the loss is not promoted. Evidence and limitations.

Metric-greedy emission (preregistered gate). Failed. Pooled cross-validated proxy DTI: uniform 0.18675, adaptive 0.18927, dense 0.13455, metric 0.13313. The metric rule needed ≥ +0.005 versus both dense and uniform; it came out −0.0014 and −0.0536, below uniform in every fold. Mechanism: the greedy rule hit its candidate-pool cap (120,000 px per fold) at 0.033–0.043 credit/px while uniform thinning emitted 3–6× fewer pixels at 2.4–4.7× the credit per pixel. Registered result.

Cross-check against the owner’s own 2026-10-02/03 findings (owner-reported, not independently verified)

Relay geometry is twice-dead: the owner’s preregistered H30-1 relay × terrain factorial screened positive (+0.0041, 4/4 folds) and then failed its fresh-draw confirmation (−0.0013, 1/4) → registered verdict “stop”; our independent H-31-01 holdout also failed its proximity control. DEM curvature/scarp is the family that works (owner factor B, +0.0253, 4/4) — the strongest external corroboration for H-31-02. Thermal/geochemical is small but consistent (D, +0.0044, 4/4, supporting H-31-04). Potential fields were inert (A, −0.0027) and strain/seismicity consistently negative (C, −0.0109, 0/4). Emission: score-ordered ~2.4 px dots at ≈2.45–3.5 % share beat score-blind thinning (+0.0034, 4/4), and the joint add-on surface passed both replicates while its predicted best corner did not — their own selection was partly screen-overfit.
