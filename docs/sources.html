The GitHub-runner bridge later downloaded and SHA-256-verified the Qfaults v2, paleogeothermal, Quaternary-volcanics, and 2 m probe ZIPs; the receipt is in external_receipt.json. The first Qfaults v2 rasterization failed because its EPSG:32611 coordinates were misread as longitude/latitude; IR-30-034 fixes ingestion. The corrected raster is recorded in derived_gdr_qfaults_v2_100m_u8.tif and the follow-up receipt: 1,117 features / 59,065 footprint pixels, with only 1 pixel more than 300 m from the training catalogue. It is not independent truth. The canonical receipt retains the original failed derivation; do not conflate the two.

Kervadec et al. · Boundary loss for highly unbalanced segmentation

Preprint first submitted in 2018; MIDL conference publication in PMLR 2019. It proposes complementing region losses with a boundary/distance term and reports medical-segmentation results. It does not establish GEMS fault-mapping gains.
