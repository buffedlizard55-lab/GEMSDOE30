Kervadec et al. · Boundary loss for highly unbalanced segmentation

Preprint first submitted in 2018; MIDL conference publication in PMLR 2019. It proposes complementing region losses with a boundary/distance term and reports medical-segmentation results. It does not establish GEMS fault-mapping gains.
