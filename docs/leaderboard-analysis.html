Mechanistic explanation

Why dotted predictions can help

For each true pixel, the scorer credits the best nearby weighted prediction; extra points around the same truth do not add proportional TP. But emitted probabilities still pay a distance-weighted FP cost. Carefully thinning a redundant line or band can retain local kernel coverage while cutting FP mass. Too much thinning loses coverage and raises FN.

This is a plausible metric mechanism. It does not establish why D2.8 might have scored 0.2600, that the exact TIFF received that score, or that thinning will generalize to the private labels.
