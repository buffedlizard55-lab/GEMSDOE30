## Why a topography-free, regional-coordinate-free subsurface lane is necessary

1. **Duplicate-convergence physics (`IR-TC2-01`):** Any pointwise tree model given `det_elev_slope` plus smooth regional interpolated coordinates (`geod_2ndinv`, `geod_shearrate`, `geod_dilaterate`, `deq_n100a15`, `ieq_n100a15`, `det_elev`) concentrates >60–80% of its emitted dots onto the same steep (>5°) range-front scarps already saturated by `G30-S19` (60,000 dots) and `GEMSDOE27`, causing `TDX-C2` (`79.09%`) and `G30-S20` (`89.58%`) to hit mandatory `duplicate-stop`.
2. **Off-catalogue geological target (NLR Rules §2; DrivenData Staff `11516/4`, `11536/2`):** Pre-2024 USGS/INGENIOUS Quaternary fault traces (`labels.tif`) were compiled largely from subaerial scarp geomorphology (`det_elev_slope`), systematically under-representing (a) **blind and concealed intrabasin/piedmont normal faults** beneath low-slope (<4°) pluvial/alluvial cover and (b) **hydrothermally altered geothermal conduit faults** where magnetite destruction suppresses raw magnetic gradient amplitude (`tmi_hg`).
3. **Newly audited unit & band-description irregularities in `training_features.tif` (label-free inspection):**
   - `tmi_hg` (band 2) has median `13.08` and p99 `186.7` (**nT/km**), whereas `tmi_vg` (band 8) has median `-0.00899` and p99 `0.777` (**nT/m** = `10^-3` nT/km). Combining `tmi_vg` and `tmi_hg` without `1000.0 * tmi_vg` unit conversion collapses vertical-derivative tilt angles by `1,000x`.
   - `tc` (band 5, labelled `"Tilt angle or total curvature"` in the reference notebook) is strictly positive (`[3.347, 46.98]`, median `18.47`, `5,164,300` finite pixels), incompatible with a signed magnetic tilt angle in radians (`[-pi/2, +pi/2]`).
   - `iso_grav_anom_hg` (band 17) is signed and zero-centered (`[-10.41, +11.45]`), whereas `iso_grav_anom_slope` (band 4) is positive (`[0.00284, 0.624]`).

## One frozen experiment (`pta1`), four fixed arms, zero tuning

All derived features use fixed physical unit floors (`EPS_MAG_NT_KM = 2.0`, `EPS_GRAV_SLOPE = 0.01`, `COVER_FLOOR_M = 50.0`), Gaussian truncation `2.0` ($\sigma = 1.0\text{ px}$ for tilt/cover/conductivity fields, $\sigma = 1.5\text{ px}$ for the gravity normal field), tangent offsets $s \in \{-4, -2, +2, +4\}\text{ px}$, and hanging-wall normal offsets $d \in \{3, 6\}\text{ px}$. Tight analytical Euclidean spatial support is $6.0 + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{13} = 11.02\text{ px} \le 12\text{ px}$ (`TIGHT_SUPPORT_PX = 12`, impulse-tested), and we declare `SUPPORT_PX = 17` (`buffer_px = 17`, `1,700 m`) so the buffered whole-component folds match `TDX-1` and `TDX-C2` identically (`12 + 3 = 15 px < 17 px`).

1. **`sub8_raw` (8 features):** Normalized subsurface bands `[rtp, tmi_hg, tmi_vg, iso_grav_anom, iso_grav_anom_slope, iso_grav_anom_vg, depth_to_base_surf, cond_surf]`.
2. **`pta_naive_sym` (18 features — Matched Unit-Naive & Dip-Symmetric Falsification Control):** `sub8_raw` plus 10 matched control features (sharing `pta_grav_tilt` and `pta_grav_hd_tdr`, plus 8 distinct control transforms using **unscaled `1.0 * tmi_vg`** to test `IR-TILT-01` unit sensitivity, **cross-normal blur** instead of gravity-tangent persistence, and **sign-erased symmetric flank curvature** $\frac{1}{2}[f(\mathbf{x}+d\hat{\mathbf{n}}) + f(\mathbf{x}-d\hat{\mathbf{n}})] - f(\mathbf{x})$ instead of signed hanging-wall minus footwall differences; exact same spatial support `SUPPORT_PX = 12` and feature count `18`).
3. **`pta1_cand` (18 features — Fixed Preregistered Candidate):** `sub8_raw` plus the 10 unit-consistent (`1000.0 * tmi_vg`) 3D Phase-Tilt Curvature, Cross-Field Concordance, Gravity-Tangent Persistence, and Signed Grauch–Hudson Hanging-Wall Dip-Asymmetry features.
4. **`core19` (19 features — Full-Stack Surface-Topography + Regional Reference Comparator):** Exact 19-band HGB comparator retained from `TDX-1` / `TDX-C2` on the identical folds, training coordinates, HGB settings, and active-domain dot budget so scarp-biased catalogue-proxy differences are reported transparently rather than omitted.
