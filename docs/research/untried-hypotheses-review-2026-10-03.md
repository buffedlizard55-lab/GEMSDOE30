# Untried geological hypothesis shortlist — 2026-10-03

**Historical snapshot — superseded by later experiments (updated 2026-10-03).** This shortlist was frozen before H-31-02b and subsequent experiments; its original Rank 1 candidate has since been tested and failed its primary spatial and strike-permutation gates. Do not treat the ranking/status below as current, and do not rerun or retune H-31-02b against the already-inspected SGMC labels. The table is retained to preserve the pre-run decision record and bounded novelty audit, not as a claim that no other team has tried the ideas. For the current active untried shortlist, verified access state, results, and promotion rules, use [`docs/hypotheses.md`](../hypotheses.md). Prior owner results are labelled as such, not treated as independent or organizer-authenticated evidence.

**Scientific target:** fault traces/segments absent from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue, not prediction of the catalogue itself. The official problem description says the training catalogue is incomplete and that experts labelled additional faults for testing; it also allows external data subject to compatible licensing and sponsor-sharing terms ([problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/)). The official About page describes scarps, displaced layers, geophysical contrasts, and elevation/remote-sensing lineaments as fault indicators ([About](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/)). These are physical motivations, not evidence that any particular feature will improve DTI.

## Selection and frozen decision rule

H-31-02 reduced scarp-orientation persistence is ranked first because (a) the 3DEP source route is verified for a test bbox, (b) a reduced, low-volume owner-derived stack can be checked locally, and (c) scarp/topographic curvature was the strongest new terrain family in an **owner-reported, non-independent** prior analysis. The latter is a prioritisation clue only; it does not update a measured score or substitute for our holdout.

Before seeing any new score, the exact transform and candidate gates are frozen in [`h31-02-scarp-preregistration-2026-10-03.md`](h31-02-scarp-preregistration-2026-10-03.md). A failed screen closes this exact reduced transform; it does not falsify all fault-scarp hypotheses or the raw 1 m DEM. No candidate earns a weekly slot from a catalogue-only proxy result. Any screen pass would still require the three standing promotion checks—spatial blocks, calibration if a learned probability surface is produced, and held-out feature perturbation—plus the qualitative help/failure review in [`verification-protocol.md`](verification-protocol.md).
