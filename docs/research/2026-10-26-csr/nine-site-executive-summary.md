## 3. PhD-Level Explanation: Why `GEMSDOE32` (`H33-B2`, `0.2778`) Topped the Nine Sites & How to Surpass `0.2778` / `0.3195` / `0.4413–0.4437`

### 3.1 Exact Differential Algebra of the `D2.8 -> r1 -> H33-B2 -> PINN` Trajectory

Per the [DrivenData Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and [Organizer Forum Clarification `t/11516`](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516), scoring evaluates pooled pixel weights over $\Omega_{\text{eval}} = \Omega \setminus K$, where known USGS/INGENIOUS fault pixels $K = \{x : \text{labels}(x) = 1\}$ are masked **pixel-exactly with zero buffer**, using $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, and the `300 m` (`3 px`) Euclidean triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d/3, 0)$:

$$\operatorname{DTI}(P) = \frac{T(P)}{0.2\,T(P) + 0.2\,F(P) + 0.8\,|G_{\text{new}}|}, \qquad T(P) = \sum_{x \in G_{\text{new}}} \tilde{P}(x) + \sum_{x \in P \setminus G_{\text{new}}} P(x)\tilde{G}_{\text{new}}(x), \qquad F(P) = \sum_{x \in P \setminus K} P(x)\bigl(1 - \tilde{G}_{\text{new}}(x)\bigr).$$

Byte-for-byte reopening (`docs/research/2026-10-26-csr/fleet-audit.json`) proves **zero new geology was added between `D2.8` (`0.2600`), `r1` (`0.2708`), and `H33-B2` (`0.2778`)** — each file is an exact distance-pruned subset of the same `h19-5` `D2.8` dot cloud:

1. **`D2.8` (`44,090` dots, `0.2600`):** `2.8 px` Poisson-disk thinning of `h19-5` (`121,131` px, `0.1922`). Because $\tilde{P}(x) = \max_{\|x-y\|\le 3} k(\|x-y\|)P(y)$ is a **spatial maximum** while $F(P)$ is a **linear sum**, spacing binary $\{0, 1\}$ dots ~`3 px` apart slashed $F(P)$ by $2.75\times$ with minimal loss in $\tilde{P}$, jumping $+0.0678$. However, `D2.8` still placed `3,891` dots at $d(x, K) = 1\text{ px}$ and `2,545` dots at $d(x, K) = 2\text{ px}$ along the blurry halos of *already-mapped* faults $K$.
2. **`r1` (`40,199` dots, `0.2708`):** Deletes the exact `3,891` dots at $d(x, K) = 1\text{ px}$. Because DrivenData masks **only** $d(x, K) = 0$ (`labels == 1`), pixels at $d(x, K) \in \{1, 2\}\text{ px}$ are **unmasked** and incur full false-positive penalty $0.2 \times 1.0 = 0.2$ each whenever no *new* hidden fault sits right beside the mapped trace! Deleting `3,891` halo dots stripped $\Delta(0.2 F) \approx -778$ denominator units while surviving dots at $d(x, K) \ge 2\text{ px}$ still extended triangular kernel reach across $d(x, K) = 1\text{ px}$.
3. **`H33-B2` (`37,654` dots, `0.2778`):** Deletes the next `2,545` dots at $d(x, K) = 2\text{ px}$, saving another $\Delta(0.2 F) \approx -509$ denominator units while surviving dots at $d(x, K) = 3\text{ px}$ still reach $d(x, K) \in [1, 2]\text{ px}$ under $R = 3\text{ px}$. Inverting $T / \operatorname{DTI}(P_i) = 0.2|P_i| + 0.8|G_{\text{new}}|$ across all three rungs yields **constant truth credit $T \approx 6,602$** to three significant figures (`|G_new| ≈ 19,213 px`, or `~12,600–19,200 px` accounting for kernel halo): all `+0.0178` gain from `D2.8` (`0.2600`) to `H33-B2` (`0.2778`) came from denominator hygiene ($\partial \operatorname{DTI}/\partial F < 0$), **not** new fault recall.
