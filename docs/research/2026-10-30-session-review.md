## 2. H33-B2: what its reported value can and cannot mean

### Verified byte lineage

Within the nine-site frozen audit, H33-B2's **owner-reported 0.2778 is the largest historical claim**. It is not a verified score or a candidate result. The restored historical GeoTIFFs reproduce the following **in-footprint masks**:

- GEMSDOE25 D2.8 has 44,090 positive cells; GEMSDOE30 has the identical in-footprint positive mask (its outside-footprint encoding differs).
- GEMSDOE31 has 40,199 positive cells and is an exact subset of that D2.8 mask, deleting 3,891 cells.
- GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 has 37,654 positive cells and is an exact subset of GEMSDOE31, deleting another 2,545 cells.

This is byte/mask lineage, not a causal score experiment. The reported 0.2778 remains **OWNER-REPORTED, UNVERIFIED**; no submission-page receipt binds it to H33-B2, and the private labels are unavailable.

### Plausible mechanism, not a measured explanation

The official metric uses a 300 m triangular kernel and weights false negatives more than false positives (α = 0.2, β = 0.8). For each hidden truth pixel, TP credit is the maximum kernel-weighted prediction rather than a sum; prediction mass away from hidden truth contributes to FP. Consequently, deleting low-value predictions can raise DTI **if** the deleted predictions remove more FP penalty than TP credit. That is a plausible pruning mechanism for the D2.8 → r1 → H33 lineage, but it is not established from the owner-reported values.

The official staff answer is important to this interpretation. The known-fault mask is pixel-exact; only those known-fault pixels are excluded. Staff also states that new-fault truth may lie within 300 m of known faults and can include corrections or modifications to existing traces. Therefore, predictions within one or two pixels of the catalogue are not automatically harmless false positives: they may be near new truth, and deleting them could remove TP as well as FP. Without the actual hidden labels and a receipt tied to each exact file, we cannot infer the sign or size of that tradeoff, calculate H33's true/false-positive contributions, or claim that pruning caused the reported increase.

**Answer:** if 0.2778 is accurate, fewer emitted cells on the same underlying H19-5/D2.8 support are a reasonable *hypothesis* for why the score improved. The repository demonstrates exact pruning lineage, not the causal score contribution. No new candidate has demonstrated a result above it.
