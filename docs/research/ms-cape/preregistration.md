## 3. Frozen Experimental Design for Primary Lane `MS-CAPE`

1. **Spatial Guard & Fold Split (`SUPPORT_PX = 20`):**
   - Stage-1 derived channels (`32` channels + `19` core bands = `51` features) have exact Euclidean stencil radius `<= 12 px` (`1,200 m`).
   - Stage-2 trace-propagation channels (`10` channels) have exact Euclidean stencil radius `<= 8 px` (`800 m`).
   - Composed receptive field is strictly bounded by `12 + 8 = 20 px` (`2,000 m`).
   - All arms are trained and evaluated on `spatial_segment_folds(labels, valid, buffer_px=20)`, withholding whole 8-connected fault components per quadrant (`60,988` total withheld positives, each scored once) with a `2,000 m` guard buffer around hidden segments.
2. **Leakage Canary Gate (Mandatory Pre-Trust Screen):**
   - Every single Stage-1 feature (`51` total: `19` official + `32` `MS-CAPE`) is tested individually in all `4` folds via both raw bidirectional ROC AUC (`max(AUC, 1 - AUC)`) and fold-trained univariate `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` ROC AUC on the held-out quadrant (`score_valid & ~visible`). Any value `> 0.90` aborts promotion immediately.
3. **Novelty Duplicate-Stop Gate (Pre-Placement Surface AND Post-Placement Dots):**
   - Audited via `src/gemsdoe30/novelty.py` against all `116` unique aligned SHA-256 rasters in `docs/research/ms-cape/registry.json` (all 9 priority sites `GEMSDOE24..36`, all local `GEMSDOE30` `docs/downloads/*.tif` files, and published hero submissions from `GEMSDOE49`, `63`, `70`, `73`, `76`).
   - Hard stop if `|Spearman| > 0.90` on continuous surface before placement OR `|Spearman| > 0.90` or `3-px dot overlap > 0.70` after placement.
4. **Budget-Capped 3-Experiment Progression (`DOT_BUDGET = 44,090` full-map / exact quadrant-proportional active count per fold):**
   - **Comparators (same folds, same active dot count per fold):**
     1. `physics_tilt8`: 8-channel Verduzco potential-field tilt/basement sub-family HGB + `3.0 px` Poisson disk.
     2. `core19`: Official 19-band HGB + `3.0 px` Poisson disk (`GEMSDOE30` canonical baseline).

     - **Experiment 1 (`ms_cape_e1`):** 51-feature Stage-1 `MS-CAPE` 2-seed regularized HGB ensemble + `3.0 px` Poisson disk (tests causal feature-stack gain).
     - **Experiment 2 (`ms_cape_e2`):** Nested out-of-quadrant Stage-2 directional trace-continuation & wash-gap-bridge stacker + `3.0 px` Poisson disk (tests causal structural trace-propagation gain).
     - **Experiment 3 (`ms_cape_e3` — Fixed Primary Candidate):** Stage-2 `MS-CAPE` Bayes-prior-recalibrated probability field + exact Minoux lazy-greedy `300 m` triangular-kernel submodular DTI cover (`min_sep_px = 2.2`, `flank_exclusion_px = 2.0` around visible faults) at exact matched `44,090` dot budget.
