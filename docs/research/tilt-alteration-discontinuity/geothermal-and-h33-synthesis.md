### 2.2 Mathematical & Physical Proof of Why `H33` Reached `0.2778` (`OWNER-REPORTED`)

Under the official pooled Distance-Weighted Tversky Index:
$$\text{DTI}(p, H) = \frac{T(p, H)}{0.2\,T(p, H) + 0.2\,F(p, H) + 0.8\,G}, \qquad G = |H|,$$
where known catalogue pixels $M$ (`60,988` cells) are zeroed out pixel-exactly before scoring (`p[M] = 0`, `y[M] = 0`), $k(d) = \max(1 - d / 300\text{ m}, 0)$,
$$T(p, H) = \sum_{g \in H} \max_{x \notin M} p(x)\,k\!\big(d(x, g)\big), \qquad F(p, H) = \sum_{x \notin M} p(x)\!\left(1 - \max_{g \in H} k\!\big(d(x, g)\big)\right).$$

Four coupled mechanisms explain every step of the `0.1922 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778` progression:

1. **Submodularity & Non-Stacking Kernel Maxima (`0.1922 → 0.2600`)**:
   Because $T(p, H)$ applies a **pointwise $\max$** (not a sum) over predictions within $R = 300\text{ m}$ (`3 px`), placing adjacent $p=1$ pixels along a ridge adds near-zero marginal $T$ once one dot already covers $g \in H$, while $F(p, H)$ sums **linearly** over every false pixel! Thinning `h19-5` from `121,131` contiguous ridge pixels to `44,090` isolated binary dots spaced at $\sim 2.8\text{–}3.0\text{ px}$ cuts $F$ roughly threefold while retaining $\sim 90\%$ of $T$.
2. **Elimination of the `≤ 200 m` Catalogue Digitization Halo (`0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778`)**:
   Faults in the USGS/INGENIOUS compilation digitized at $1:24{,}000\text{–}1:100{,}000$ rasterize to 1-pixel-wide (`100 m`) polylines $M$, whereas their topographic/magnetic anomaly ridges (`det_elev_slope`, `tmi_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_hg`) are $300\text{–}600\text{ m}$ wide. Any slope/gradient detector therefore fires strongly at $d(x, M) \in (0, 200\text{ m}]$ alongside *already-mapped* faults. Because the organizer masks $M$ **pixel-exactly** (1 px wide), predictions at $1\text{–}2\text{ px}$ are **not masked** and pay full $F = 1.0$ penalty unless a *second* unmapped strand sits $100\text{–}200\text{ m}$ away (physically rare; implied differential hit rate from `D2.8 → r1 → H33` is `0.000–0.004` hits/dot vs `0.023` for uniform random dots!). Moreover, if a genuine unmapped tip branches from $M$, a dot at $d(x, M) = 3\text{ px}$ ($300\text{ m}$) still reaches back to $d = 1\text{ px}$ ($k(200\text{ m}) = 1/3$) and $d = 2\text{ px}$ ($k(100\text{ m}) = 2/3$). Deleting the `6,436` dots at $d(x, M) \le 2\text{ px}$ therefore removes almost pure $F$ waste (`-6,436` false dots $\implies -1,287$ denominator units $\implies +0.0178$ DTI!).
3. **Portal `[0, 1]` Safety (`-zeros`)**:
   Encoding outside-footprint cells as `0.0` (`float32`, finite `[0, 1]` across all `12,279,160` raster cells) avoids any portal validator failure when `np.min(arr) >= 0` and `np.max(arr) <= 1` are checked prior to nodata masking.

### 2.3 Why `H33` Plateaus at `~0.278` — and How a Model Can Score Higher (`> 0.2778` Toward `0.4413`)

Label-blind calibration from `GEMSDOE13`'s exact 5-px square lattice (`0.0904` owner-reported $\implies$ hidden new-fault pixel density $\rho \approx 0.244\%$, `PROJECTION`) shows that `D2.8`, `r1`, `H33`, and `PINN` all recover the **same $\approx 40.5\%$ of hidden fault truth** (`~0.134` hits/dot for `H33`). Meanwhile, the live public leader (`#1 xiaofanhu 0.4413`, `OFFICIAL-LEADERBOARD-SNAPSHOT 2026-10-25`) requires covering **$\approx 60\%\text{–}70\%$ of hidden fault truth** at higher precision!

Why did `h19-5` / `H33` miss the remaining **$\sim 60\%$ of expert-mapped new faults**, AND why did three consecutive repository lanes (`G30-S20`, `TDX-C2`, `G30-LSM`) trigger `duplicate-stop` (`79%–90%` 3-px dot overlap with `G30-S19`)?

1. **Positive-Unlabeled Topographic-Observability Censoring**:
   By Bayes' theorem for censored geological mapping:
   $$P(\text{catalogue} = 1 \mid x) = P(\text{fault} = 1 \mid x)\cdot o(x),$$
   where observability $o(x) = P(\text{mapped in USGS/INGENIOUS} \mid \text{fault} = 1, x)$ approaches `1` on steep exposed bedrock range fronts (`det_elev_slope` high) and approaches `0` in **alluvial basins, pediment covers, intrabasin stepovers, and smectite-capped blind geothermal systems** — even though **39%–75% of Great Basin geothermal systems are structurally blind** with no surface fault scarp (Faulds & Hinz 2015)!
2. **Coordinate Memorization via `core19` Regional Levels**:
   `core19` contains 12 long-wavelength regional level fields (`det_elev`, `iso_grav_anom`, `tmi`, `mag_anom`, `rtp`, `depth_to_base_surf`, `cond_surf`, `geod_*` at 20–50 km, `deq/ieq` at 100 km). Decision trees fit on `labels.tif` use these smooth fields as 2-D spatial coordinates $(x, y)$ memorizing *already-mapped* mountain fronts: `G30-LSM` proved that even without a proximity input, an in-sample HGB fit with `core19` puts **70% of its dots inside `200 m – 1 km` of mapped faults** and only **6% beyond `3 km`**, crowding the exact barren collar already saturated by `G30-S19`!

**To break past `0.2778` toward `0.40+` without lane collision, two simultaneous advances are required:**
1. **Translation-Invariant Multi-Physics Discontinuity & Hydrothermal Alteration Representation**: Replace the 12 regional coordinate-proxy bands of `core19` with strictly local, amplitude-normalized geophysical contact-depth operators (Salem–Blakely Tilt-Depth, 150 m upward-continuation vertical persistence), lithology-normalized K–Th–U hydrothermal alteration residuals (Saunders–Dickson $K_d$, Efimov $F$-parameter), MT clay-cap lateral conductance boundaries, 1 m LiDAR dip-slip face-polarity asymmetry, and non-equilibrium geothermometer / paleogeothermal sinter upflow indicators.
2. **Observability-Decensored Distance-Collar Allocation**: Enforce `0%` budget at $d(x, M) \le 200\text{ m}$ (H33 rule) AND cap the saturated $200\text{ m} - 1\text{ km}$ near-catalogue collar at `30%`, dedicating `70%` of the `38,000`-dot budget (`40%` in `1–3 km` stepovers/pediments, `30%` in `>3 km` concealed basins & unmapped interiors) ranked by out-of-fold cross-quadrant ensemble belief.
