### Why H33-B2 is the highest owner-reported value

The pixel-level lineage is verified; the causal score story is not. The archived audit records GEMSDOE25/GEMSDOE30 as value-identical, a 3,891-dot pruning step from D2.8 to an r1 intermediate, then removal of 2,545 dots to produce 37,654-dot H33-B2. GEMSDOE36 PINN adds 1,200 dots to H33-B2. The selected 37,660-dot GEMSDOE28 file is a different raster from the G28 r1 intermediate associated with the G31 alias.

A plausible mechanism is reduced false-positive cost when dots near mapped traces add little hidden-fault credit; however the hidden organizer labels and an organizer receipt for these owner values are unavailable. We cannot know which pruned dots were true positives, prove pruning caused any score change, or conclude that the reported sequence represents “denominator hygiene at constant truth credit.” This is file lineage, not independent geological discovery or a score explanation.
