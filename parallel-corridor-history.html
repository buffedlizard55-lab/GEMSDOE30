How to make a submission into the contest

  1. Get organizer data access. Log in to DrivenData and download training_features.tif, labels.tif, and sample_submission.tif from the competition data tab (requires login).
  2. Prepare the data. bash scripts/download_competition_data.sh into data/, then python scripts/prepare_data.py.
  3. Build a candidate. Reuse src/gemsdoe30.holdout and src/gemsdoe30.submission (no private forks). Holdout = hide whole fault segments with a buffer, derive catalogue features only from visible faults, mask visible faults pixel-exactly, score pooled DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel).
  4. Validate the file. python scripts/validate_submission.py CANDIDATE.tif --template data/raw/sample_submission.tif — requires 1 band float32, EPSG:32611, template shape/transform, finite [0,1] inside footprint, NaN/nodata outside.
  5. Check uniqueness. Rank-correlation ≤0.90 and ≤70% of dots within 3 px of any one registry raster, on the surface before placement AND on the final dots.
  6. Holdout gate. The candidate must beat the current holdout best with a positive paired 95% CI before a weekly slot is selected.
  7. Submit. Upload the single-band GeoTIFF (or zip) at the DrivenData submission page with a unique name and a note (≤140 chars). Weekly cap: up to 3 feedback submissions/week; 1 final selected entry. Deadline per NLR rules PDF: 5:00 p.m. ET / 22:00 UTC on Dec. 3, 2026.
Common upload error fixed by our artifacts: "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" — every featured GeoTIFF is finite [0.0, 1.0] inside the scoring footprint (NaN only outside, as the published format permits).

Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778 — and whether we can beat 0.2778 / 0.4413

Exact metric calculus: the pooled Distance-Tversky Index is DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2 FP_w + 0.8 FN_w) with TP_w max-pooled per truth pixel over a 300 m (3 px) triangular kernel and FP_w summed linearly across unmasked non-truth pixels. DrivenData staff (threads 11516/11536) confirmed known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked pixel-exactly (labels == 1, zero buffer) while hidden splays may sit <300 m from mapped traces.

Why 0.2778: GEMSDOE32 H33 deletes the 2,545 dots at 0 < d<sub>cat ≤ 2 px from GEMSDOE28's 40,199-dot base, leaving 37,654 dots. Under pixel-exact masking, the 1–2 px flank dots are unmasked flank spillover costing ≈0.2·FP_w each; dots at d<sub>cat = 3 px still reach hidden splays via the 300 m kernel. Byte-exact reconstruction (0 mismatching pixels) proves H33 is a pruned G28, not new signal.

Can we beat 0.2778 / 0.4413? Not verifiably without organizer labels. The 0.24–0.2778 family plateaus because 70–81% of dots sit on steep scarps, missing blind faults under Quaternary playas/fans. Beating it requires higher recall on concealed intrabasin structure without flooding background FP_w — plus a holdout pass before spending a slot. Live public leader (2026-10-25): 0.4413.
