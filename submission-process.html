02 / Official competition flow

When a future candidate is genuinely ready.

  1. Read the official problem page and rules.

    Use the DrivenData problem specification and the NLR Official Rules. Confirm live deadlines, eligibility, permitted data, and the actual portal instructions there; the repository does not invent a current deadline.

  2. Resolve the research and lane gates first.

    Before any experiment, obtain the assigned method lane and complete parallel registry; freeze a pre-placement continuous-surface registry; use whole-segment buffered holdout, exact visible-pixel masking, shared evaluator/writer, 2,000 paired geographic-block bootstrap draws, and single-feature leakage canaries. Stop for Spearman > 0.90 or >70% of dots within 3 px of a registered raster before or after placement.

  3. Promote and choose a slot as separate decisions.

    A future candidate must pass its exact-file local validator and the predeclared HOLDOUT-DTI gate against its assigned comparator(s). The existing msdb_edge − msdb_poisson interval is inconclusive and is not a promotion gate; it does not justify adding a new gate retroactively. Only a separate, authorized selector can choose or spend a weekly submission slot. The official rules describe a rolling weekly submission limit and a single final entry across the prize phases; re-check the official document for the controlling text.

  4. Upload only the exact authorized TIFF through the official portal.

    Do not upload JSON sidecars, HTML, score ledgers, or public reference rasters. The repository has no portal uploader and has not accessed the private submission page.

  5. Retain the organizer receipt and bind it to exact bytes.

    Record the official receipt, file identity, timestamp, and organizer-reported score. Only then may the score be labelled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Without that receipt, retain the label OWNER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED.
