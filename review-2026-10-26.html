Why H33 plausibly improved, and what is not established

The stored recipe reconstruction and prior forensic review report H33 as the solo d2.8 dot set minus dots within two pixels of catalogue faults. This is postprocessing lineage, not evidence for a new geothermal-vent detector. Under the repository's max-pooled triangular kernel, clustered predictions can add penalty mass without proportional recovery; thinning and near-catalogue exclusion can improve this trade-off. However, exclusion can also discard real unmapped parallel strands. Exact reconstruction establishes an operation, not why hidden-label performance changed. Prior prose asserting that all removed points were false positives or retained a specific hidden-label recall is unsupported without hidden labels and is not adopted here.

Can we beat the owner's H33 claim? Possibly, but neither a catalogue-proxy win nor mathematical motivation establishes that. A better ranker must recover additional hidden geometry at acceptable false-positive cost. No projection is a score. Mapping fault geometry is this competition's immediate target; identifying active geothermal vents is a separate inference requiring independent thermal/flow evidence.
