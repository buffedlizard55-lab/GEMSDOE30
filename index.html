03 / Historical lesson

H33 is a pruning lesson, not independent geology.

Educational byte-level reconstruction reproduced H33's 37,654-dot support exactly from the 40,199-dot GEMSDOE28 base by removing dots at catalogue distance ≤2 pixels. Nearby predictions share max-kernel truth credit, so redundant support can add penalty without adding coverage.
