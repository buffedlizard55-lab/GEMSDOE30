What this project is doing

Fit the model to the score, test the geology.

Metric/loss, training, OOF stitching, and safe file-building plumbing passed disposable synthetic smoke tests. Real-data spatial holdouts have since run on owner-mirror rasters: the four-arm pilot failed its frozen screen, the paired loss ablation screened positive on seed 30 but failed its fresh-seed (31) confirmation, and the metric-emission gate failed. Screen results are not competition scores and synthetic checks are not holdout evidence.

01 · Scoring geometry

Reward proximity, not just pixel coincidence

A 300 m triangular kernel gives a one-pixel near miss partial credit. The combined loss adds a differentiable distance-weighted term to regional soft-Tversky; it does not replace the regional objective.

02 · Scientific tests

Five active, falsifiable hypotheses

The untried shortlist covers K–Th–U radiometric alteration, geothermometer discordance, volcanic-vent alignment, drainage response, and conditional Qfaults gap completion. Expected gains are planning priors—not measured scores—and each has a spatial-holdout gate.

03 · Submission safety

Fail closed on bad GeoTIFFs

The validator checks one float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, exact template transform/shape, finite in-range values inside the footprint, and both outside conventions — NaN (official sample convention) or finite 0.0 (locally checked alternative; portal acceptance unverified). The --portal-safe mode additionally requires every cell in the raster to be finite and in [0, 1].
