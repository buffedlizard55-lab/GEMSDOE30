Four distinct research mechanisms.

Qualitative priorities only.
No score forecasts or new experiments.

1. Regime-gated experts

Combine piedmont scarps with a cover-blind magnetic/gravity/MT expert; gate by a preregistered regime. Mimics: fan scarps, lava fronts and lithology. Cached layers; moderate modeling cost. Similar gating has been discussed in parallel work.

2. Buried contact discordance

Project geology-unit contacts beneath alluvium and compare with independent geophysical edges. Mimics: unfaulted unconformities. New geology data and moderate GIS work; exclude fault-derived tables.

3. Drainage deflection

Find repeated channel offsets/knickpoint alignments coincident with an independent geophysical edge. Mimics: terraces, roads and lava dams. Requires DEM conditioning and catchments.

4. Alteration persistence

Test GeoDAWN K/Th/U log-ratio residuals along coherent lines with a survey-block null. Mimics: soil, rock and flight-line seams. Cached radiometry; survey masks still needed.

Protocol & sources

What happens before a future slot.

  1. Obtain a single assigned scientific lane and an explicit resolution of the literal registry constraint; do not reduce the budget merely to evade it.
  2. Pre-register whole-segment, spatially buffered hide-and-recover folds; use visible faults only for catalogue-derived features, and audit each feature for leakage.
  3. Beat the current same-protocol control with uncertainty, check surface and final dots against the complete current registry, and validate the exact saved bytes.
  4. Require separate slot-selector approval. A missing gate, incomplete inventory or local format pass is not an upload authorization.
