New 2026-10-03 (Session 30): the emitter is solved, the frame was lying — EDGE, IR-30-039, and four hypotheses a surface catalogue cannot contain

1) EDGE: a metric-exact emitter that beats every belief-ordered baseline on the same field (registered JSON · preregistration). The published index pays a dot only when its kernel credit clears k > 0.2·s/(1−0.2·s) (0.055 at the owner-reported 0.2600 operating point, 0.068 at the public leader's 0.3195), so emission is a coverage problem, not a calibration problem. src/gemsdoe30/emitter_opt.py implements the expected-marginal-credit greedy dT(x) = Σδ π(x+δ)·max(0, k(δ) − C(x+δ)) and stops exactly at that threshold. On the whole-catalogue component holdout (seed 31: 20,870 stand-in hidden pixels, 40,118 known pixels masked from every metric term; four spatial quadrant folds; leave-one-fold-out parameter choice per family), EDGE scored 0.18754 vs the best other family's 0.13848 on the proximity field (+0.04906, 4/4 folds), 0.15784 vs 0.14015 on external evidence (+0.01768, 3/4), and 0.18368 vs 0.14014 on the hybrid field (+0.04354, 3/4). Uniform thinning (≈0.125), dense quantile emission (0.060–0.074) and the previously shipped belief-ordered rule (0.031–0.060) were beaten decisively in the same runs. Credit bookkeeping is verified against an independent from-scratch credit pass (relative error 1.95e-7). Caveats, stated up front: this frame's stand-in "hidden" truth is half of the same catalogue, so the margins are proxy margins; the sweeps stopped at an 80,000-dot cap on two fields (a 160,000-dot convergence run is registered separately); and these absolute values are not comparable with the earlier registered 0.18675/0.18927 pair, which was computed on a different frame whose inputs are not present in this environment.

2) IR-30-039 — the catalogue proxy ranks the owner's dot-thinning family backwards. Measured this session on hash-verified bytes: full-catalogue proxy DTI is 0.16635 for the 121,131-dot parent, 0.17193 for the 60,069-dot d1.5 step, 0.16177 for the 44,090-dot d2.8 step — while the owner-reported scores for the same three artifacts rise 0.1922 → 0.2477 → 0.2600. The official metric deletes USGS/INGENIOUS pixels from every term, so a catalogue frame pays for exactly the dots the competition removes. Consequence: catalogue-derived gates remain mandatory degenerate-failure screens but can no longer adjudicate emission design or promote an artifact; the archived negative verdicts under that frame are screen failures inside a biased frame, while the independent USGS-SGMC novelty frames in those same reports remain valid. Full analysis: Highest-score study.

3) H-34 register — four candidates aimed where a surface catalogue is structurally blind (hypotheses). Ranked by expected index gain and cost, each naming layers, physical signature, why it can catch a fault absent from USGS/INGENIOUS, its difference from everything already implemented here, the official free source with its access state checked today, and a falsifiable blocked-holdout gate: H-34-01 substrate-concealment prior from USGS SGMC map-unit polygons (data already on disk, +0.002…+0.015, next action); H-34-02 potential-field depth lineaments (tilt derivative + Euler deconvolution on the GeoDAWN magnetic grids, +0.002…+0.012); H-34-03 drainage χ-transform disequilibrium and ksn (USGS NHD + 3DEP, +0.002…+0.010, blocked until the clipped official product is on disk); H-34-04 heat-flow anomalies and borehole-gradient discontinuities (IHFC Global Heat Flow Database release 2024, +0.001…+0.008, power-check first). Each source was re-verified today and recorded in the source register. None is validated; none may consume a weekly slot.

4) The 0.3195 gap is a coverage gap, and it is now quantified. At s = 0.3195 a dot pays iff it lands within ≈280 m of a scored truth pixel; under the owner-model hidden mass (12,691 px) the leader's statistics require T = 3,466 + 0.0683·F, i.e. roughly one emitted dot in eight to one in five within 300 m of a hidden fault — 27–34 % weighted coverage of the hidden truth. With perfect knowledge the same metric ceilings near 0.78, so the remaining gap is evidence, not emission. The unique strategy proposed: EDGE over a belief field that fuses near-trace structural priors, external evidence and (new) concealment, with a quantile-floor hedging rule registered as a decision rule. See why the 0.2600 file looks like that.

1) 6.38× clumping confounder (`IR-30-033`) & emitter-order fix (`IR-30-031`). Audit of earlier top-N holdouts (basement_edge_holdout.py, vent_corridor_holdout.py) revealed that top_n_emission emitted contiguous blobs (median_nn_px = 1.0 px, 99.95 % ≤ 3 px) whereas random_near_matched emitted dispersed dots (median_nn_px = 5.10–8.94 px). In addition, emission.py's np.lexsort key order was fixed so Poisson-disk thinning visits pixels in descending score order rather than raster-major order (IR-30-031).

2) Placement-policy tests (H-33-01) & 1.5 km coherence gate re-test (H-32-05b, fixing IR-30-029). On held-out catalogue components at N = 15,000 Poisson-thinned dots, near-splay placement (300–1,500 m) scores 0.07234 (screen) and 0.07434 (confirm) — 2.7× uniform-domain placement (0.02674) and 18.6× far-basin placement (0.00389). On the OOF model score surface at 40,000 dots (h33-01-placement-policy-holdout.md), whole-domain score-first thinning (0.07608) beats blind random by +35 % and beats band-stratified quota overrides (0.0721–0.0725), proving the next lever must improve the score field itself. Re-gating the basement step ridge at the 90th percentile of 1.5 km structure-tensor coherence (coherence ≥ 0.6545, selecting 10.0 % of footprint pixels vs 99.65 % in IR-30-029) improves the Poisson-thinned basement score on both screen (0.02380 → 0.02574) and confirm (0.02566 → 0.02933), but remains below its spacing+distance-matched control (0.03660 / 0.03655; falsified).

01 · Scoring geometry

Reward proximity, not just pixel coincidence

A 300 m triangular kernel gives a one-pixel near miss partial credit. The combined loss adds a differentiable distance-weighted term to regional soft-Tversky; it does not replace the regional objective.

03 · Submission safety

Fail closed on bad GeoTIFFs

The validator checks one float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, exact template transform/shape, finite [0, 1] values inside the footprint, and null/NaN outside. This is the published-format default; an all-finite zero-outside variant is a nonstandard diagnostic only and fails the published-format check.
