01 · Scoring geometry

Reward proximity, not just pixel coincidence

A 300 m triangular kernel gives a one-pixel near miss partial credit. The combined loss adds a differentiable distance-weighted term to regional soft-Tversky; it does not replace the regional objective.

03 · Submission safety

Fail closed on bad GeoTIFFs

The validator checks one float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, exact template transform/shape, finite in-range values inside the footprint, and both outside conventions — NaN (template copy) or finite 0.0 (locally checked alternative; portal acceptance unverified). The --portal-safe mode additionally requires every cell in the raster to be finite and in [0, 1].
