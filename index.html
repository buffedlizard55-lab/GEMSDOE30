01 / What went wrong, precisely

“No proximity input” is not “no proximity”.

  • The model has no distance-to-fault feature, yet 71% of its dots lie 200 m–1 km from mapped faults and only 6% beyond 3 km: catalogue-trained habitat models learn that fault-like pixels sit next to faults.
  • That is where proximity-based lanes (G30-S19, GEMSDOE27/28 EDGE variants) already put their dots, so >70% of ours fall within 3 px of theirs (chance level 14–18%).
  • Owner-reported leaderboard differentials say this zone is unproductive (near-catalogue additions earned ≤ random), so the duplicate-stop also protected a weekly slot.
  • On the independent SGMC frame LSM42 trailed H33 at equal budget: -0.0215 [-0.0353, -0.0057] (frame is not a validated leaderboard proxy).

04 / Why H33 scored 0.2778

Same signal, fewer wasted dots.

  • H33 = GEMSDOE28 r1-solo ∧ (distance > 2 px), byte-verified; r1 = D2.8 ∧ (distance > 1 px); D2.8 ⊂ GEMSDOE19 h19-5 support.
  • An exact 5-px lattice (owner-reported 0.0904) calibrates the hidden new-fault density at ≈0.244% of pixels (PROJECTION). D2.8, r1, H33 and PINN all cover the same ≈40.5% of it; H33 simply wastes the fewest dots (implied 0.134 hits/dot, 5.8× random).
  • The 6,436 pruned near-catalogue dots had implied hit rate ≈0.002; gap-closure and PINN additions near faults earned ≤ random.
