Under the 300 m (3 px) max-pooled kernel, clumped pixels cannibalize each other's true-positive footprint: applying r = 3.0 px Poisson-disk thinning on the exact same basement feature surface raises DTI from 0.00373 → 0.02380 (6.38× multiplier).

Fit the model to the score, test the geology.

Screen results are not competition scores and synthetic checks are not holdout evidence.

01 · Scoring geometry

Reward proximity, not just pixel coincidence

A 300 m triangular kernel gives a one-pixel near miss partial credit. The combined loss adds a differentiable distance-weighted term to regional soft-Tversky; it does not replace the regional objective.

Four ranked, falsifiable hypotheses

03 · Submission safety

Fail closed on bad GeoTIFFs

The validator checks one float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, exact template transform/shape, finite in-range values inside the footprint, and both outside conventions — NaN (template copy) or finite 0.0 (portal-safe upload). The --portal-safe mode additionally requires every cell in the raster to be finite and in [0, 1].
