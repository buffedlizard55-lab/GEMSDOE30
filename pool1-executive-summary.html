Why did GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 happen — and can we beat it?

The 0.2778 value is an unverified owner report, not an organizer receipt. Removing near-catalogue halo dots could reduce false-positive cost, but without hidden labels we cannot know whether the removed dots were false or true positives, prove constant true-positive credit, or attribute the reported value to pruning. This is verified raster lineage, not a demonstrated causal score explanation or new geological discovery.
