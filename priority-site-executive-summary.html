03 / What actually explains the H33 claim

Sparse, de-redundant emission can help; copying it cannot.

The distance-weighted Tversky metric credits each truth pixel through a local maximum but accumulates false-positive mass across emitted pixels. Thus 300 m-scale spacing and removal of catalogue-adjacent redundant dots can improve a score if lost weighted coverage is smaller than false-positive penalty saved. This is a mechanism-level explanation of the exact H28→H33 transformation, not proof of an organizer score, a generalizable geological signal, or a way to certify a copied file.

To move beyond the historical family, improve the belief surface with independently motivated fault physics, then test it using buffered whole-segment hide-and-recover folds, feature canaries, count-matched controls, and the pre-placement/final-dot novelty checks.

04 / What H33 establishes — and what it does not

Pruning is a measured lineage fact, not a verified score receipt.

The audited H33 support is exactly the audited H28 reference after removing catalogue-distance <= 2 px dots (zero support mismatches).

With a 300 m distance-weighted Tversky metric, redundant nearby emissions can add false-positive mass faster than they add max-pooled truth coverage. Catalogue-flank pruning therefore plausibly improved the reported result, conditional on the reported score attribution being correct.

Boundary: No organizer submission receipt proves the 0.2778 score-to-file association, and the raster relationship does not establish a new independent prediction surface or a generalizable geological mechanism.

Could a distinct strategy improve?

A genuinely distinct geological belief surface could plausibly improve a future artifact, but this repository has no evidence that it will exceed 0.2778 or any current leaderboard value; no numerical competition forecast is made.

The linked scientific review records an organizer-published public snapshot dated 2026-10-25 with #1 = 0.4413. This summary does not refresh or extrapolate it.

Evidence required before an upload

  • Pre-register a distinct physical mechanism, non-fault mimic, source/license/coverage checks, and a count-matched comparator.
  • Use buffered whole-segment spatial holdout with visible-fault-only catalogue features, pixel-exact visible-fault masking, and pooled 300 m HOLDOUT-DTI with CI.
  • Run individual feature leakage canaries; AUC > 0.90 is leakage until disproven.
  • Stop on either continuous-surface Spearman > 0.90 or >70% final-dot overlap within 3 px against the observed registry.
  • Pass format, paired-improvement, novelty, and readiness gates; only then let a separate slot selector decide.
