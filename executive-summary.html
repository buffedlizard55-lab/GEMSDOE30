Step by step

Using the official “New submission” form

  1. Download the TIF.

    Click the download button above and save GEMSDOE30-nearslay-annulus-d3-offcat-20261025T235837Z-662af003-nan.tif. Do not edit, clip, resize, or re-encode it: any edit changes the SHA-256 and voids the validation receipt.

  2. Open the official form.

    In an authorized DrivenData account on the official competition page, go to “New submission” → “File to submit”.

  3. Attach the file.

    Choose the single-band GeoTIFF (.tif). This repository does not authenticate, upload, or consume a weekly slot; that step belongs to you, in your account.

  4. Paste the note.

    Paste the copied submission note into “Note (optional)” so the submission can be told apart later (e.g. “clustering with k=25”).

  5. Keep the receipt.

    Record the submission ID, returned score, file SHA-256 and timestamp. Only a submission-page receipt is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED; holdout deltas and projections are never written as scores.
