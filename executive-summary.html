Range-error root cause & fix

Why "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" happened—and why both twins are clean.

Direct byte inspection of training_features.tif found -3.4028235e+38 nodata sentinels on 3,061 in-footprint cells (3,073 on band 6 tc), and band 1 marks only 5,165,852 cells finite vs. 5,167,373 scored footprint cells in sample_submission.tif. Pipelines that derive masks from band 1 or pass raw sentinels emit in-footprint NaN or -3.4e38, triggering rejection.

MS-CAPE locks validity strictly to sample_submission.tif, imputes sensor gaps via training-fold medians, verifies min=0.0, max=1.0, in_footprint_nan=0 on reopened GeoTIFFs, and ships both -nan.tif and all-finite -zeros.tif twins.
