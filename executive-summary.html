Exactly how a later submission would work.

  1. Pass project research gates.

    The exact artifact must beat a comparable holdout best, clear all feature canaries, pass both novelty stages against a complete registry, and match the template. Unknown blocks promotion.

  2. Use the separate slot selector.

    Verify the current weekly allowance and required entrant/rules evidence before allocating a slot. Research promotion is not an upload permission.

  3. Download the selected .tif, not this guide.

    The authorized selected artifact must have its own one-click link, hash-bound automated verdict, unique name and short note. Do not use an archived reference, JSON, HTML or either negative candidate here.

  4. Use the official “New submission” form.

    In an authorized account on the official competition, choose the selected single-band GeoTIFF under “File to submit” and paste its note. The repository does not authenticate, upload, or use a weekly slot.

  5. Retain the organizer receipt.

    Record the returned submission ID, file SHA-256, score and timestamp before calling any result ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Never relabel HOLDOUT-DTI or a projection as a competition score.
