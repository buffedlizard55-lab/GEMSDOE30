PhD note: why GEMSDOE32 (H33-B2, 0.2778) led the nine sites — and why it is not a submission you should copy.

The progression 0.1922 → 0.2477 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 is a single subset chain under DrivenData's zero-buffer known-fault mask: h19-5 → D1.5 → D2.8 (GEMSDOE25 = GEMSDOE30) → r1 (GEMSDOE28 = GEMSDOE31) → H33-B2 (GEMSDOE32, prunes the d_cat ∈ {1, 2} px dots next). Deleting near-catalogue dots removes ≈ 0.2 × FP_w denominator penalty while surviving dots at d_cat = 3 px still reach hidden splays through the 300 m triangular kernel — truth coverage stayed ~invariant, so DTI rose. It plateaus near ~0.28 because denominator pruning alone cannot raise concealed-fault recall; beating 0.3195 / 0.4413 requires adding genuinely new concealed intrabasin faults (basin interiors, playas, alluvial fans), which CTD-1 was built to target but did not yet achieve on the holdout proxy. H33-B2's bytes are published; resubmitting them is neither unique nor slot-worthy.
