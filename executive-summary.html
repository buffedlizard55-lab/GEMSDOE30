03 / Competition submission protocol

How weekly slot selection & portal upload work.

  1. Verify automated receipts (`exit 0`).

    Run .venv/bin/python scripts/verify_current_run.py to re-verify SHA-256 hashes, [0, 1] bounds, 167-raster novelty, and 2,000-draw paired spatial bootstrap CIs directly from disk.

  2. Separate weekly-slot selector (`3 submissions / week` cap).

    Under NLR Official Rules §3.4 (96647.pdf), teams have at most 3 submissions per rolling week and select 1 final submission before December 3, 2026. No slot was selected or spent by this repository.

  3. Download the raw .tif binary only.

    Never upload JSON sidecars, HTML pages, or copied registry files. Upload a single-band float32 GeoTIFF directly.

  4. Record organizer receipts honestly.

    Only scores copied from an official DrivenData submission receipt may be labeled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED; local HOLDOUT-DTI values are never projected onto leaderboard scores.

04 / Portal range-error prevention

Strict [0, 1] clipping & sentinel sanitization.

training_features.tif contains -3.4028235e+38 float32 nodata sentinels at 3,061 in-footprint pixels. Deriving masks from raw feature bands rather than sample_submission.tif or writing unclipped logits can trigger DrivenData's "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" rejection.

src/gemsdoe30/msdb.py sanitizes all non-finite cells within exact 12.73 px Euclidean reach, and src/gemsdoe30/submission.py reopens the written GeoTIFF to verify every in-footprint pixel lies strictly in [0.0, 1.0].
