How to make a submission into the contest

  1. Get the template. Download sample_submission.tif from the DrivenData competition data page (login required): https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/data/. Your prediction must match its CRS (EPSG:32611), shape (3730×3292), and geotransform (100 m, north-up).
  2. Produce a single-band float32 GeoTIFF. Values finite and in [0, 1] inside the footprint; null/NaN outside the footprint. Do not upload zeros outside — that triggers "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]". Our candidate below follows this convention.
  3. Validate locally. Run python scripts/validate_submission.py yourfile.tif --template data/raw/sample_submission.tif. All 12 checks must pass before you consider uploading.
  4. Name and note. Use a unique run name (our manifest records GEMSDOE30-geothermal-corridor-offcat-d3) and a submission note of at most 140 characters describing the method (e.g. our note, 110 chars: "Geothermal corridor off-cat: Q-volcanics x paleo-springs x 2m probes, SGMC-gated, 3px Poisson disk, 40000 dots").
  5. Upload on the submission page. "New submission → File to submit → choose your .tif (or a .zip containing one GeoTIFF) → Note (optional) → Submit." One final selection is scored on both label sets; up to three feedback submissions per week are allowed.
