Root cause & automated prevention.

training_features.tif stores -3.4028235e+38 float32 nodata sentinels on 3,061 pixels inside the valid scoring footprint (3,073 on tc; IR-30-044 / IR-PTA-02). Masking from feature bands instead of np.isfinite(sample_submission.tif) or leaking unscaled logits/NaNs inside those 3,061 cells triggers the portal range rejection.

src/gemsdoe30/submission.py derives the valid mask exclusively from sample_submission.tif, enforces finite float32 values strictly in [0.0, 1.0] on all 5,167,373 footprint cells, writes NaN nodata outside, zeroes known catalogue pixels (labels == 1), and re-opens the written GeoTIFF from disk before publishing.
